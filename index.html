01 · About

Biology first. Data with purpose.

My background is in Bioinformatics & Systems Biology, with experience spanning RNA sequencing, multi-omics integration, computational immunology and translational research. I enjoy the point where messy biological data becomes a testable, interpretable story.

My work has included cancer transcriptomics, hereditary disease genomics, metabolomics, kidney allocation research and HLA compatibility. I care about reproducibility, thoughtful statistics and making computational results understandable to the people who need to use them.
